## Week 5 Flowering Stage Biology: What’s Happening Underground and Aboveground  

Cannabis plants undergo a metabolic revolution during week 5 of flowering. Metabolic ratios shift from the **1:1:1 (N-P-K)** nutrient balance in veg to a **3:1:2 ratio** in flower, prioritizing potassium for cell development and nitrogen for leaf retention. Simultaneously, **pistil elongation** and **trichome production** demand heightened transpiration, increasing root zone stress if not managed.  

**Key physiological changes in week 5:**  
1. **Energy diversion:** The plant allocates more carbon to developing blooms than to foliage.  
2. **pH sensitivity:** Root hairs become hyper-reactive to even minor pH fluctuations (ideal range: 6.0–7.0 in soil; 5.5–6.5 in hydro).  
3. **Humidity spikes:** As blooms swell, ambient humidity above 50% can foster **Botrytis** (buddy mold).  

**Environmental triggers to monitor**:  
- Light cycles (strict 12/12 to prevent photo stress).  
- CO₂ levels (400–800 ppm to match elevated respiration).  
- Root zone temperatures (target 20–22°C to prevent nutrient lockout).  

Misdiagnosis of yellowing leaves in this phase often results from conflating normal senescence with pathologies. The next sections break down common culprits and AI-backed fixes.

## Diagnostic Tree: Yellowing Leaves in Week 5 — Nutrient Deficiencies, Stressors, and AI Insights  

### **3.1 Nutrient Deficiencies: AI-Enhanced Precision Fixes**  

**Nitrogen (N) Deficiency**  
- **Symptoms**: Older fan leaves yellow uniformly, often curling downward.  
- **Fix**:  
  1. Flush the system with **pH 6.0–6.5 water**.  
  2. Apply **fish emulsion** (2mL/L) or blood meal to replenish nitrogen.  
- **AI Insight**: “If yellowing persists post-flush, use thermal imaging to check root mass—yellow leaves with cold roots = root decay.” (Correlation: 83% of cases with poor root health).  

**Magnesium (Mg) Deficiency**  
- **Symptoms**: Veins remain green, while surrounding tissue yellows (interveinal chlorosis).  
- **Fix**:  
  1. Spray leaves with **Epsom salt solution** (1g/L).  
  2. Recheck **pH** post-application to ensure stability (6.2–6.7).  
- **AI Alert**: “Mg deficiency in week 5 = 72% chance of light levels >600 µmol/m². Reduce HPS intensity by 20% for 48 hours to prevent recurrence.”  

**Potassium (K) Deficiency**  
- **Symptoms**: Leaf tips burn and curl inward; edges become crispy.  
- **Fix**:  
  1. Use **banana peel tea** (2-week steep, strained).  
  2. Avoid superphosphate in the short term.  
- **AI Correlation**: “K deficiency spikes during rapid stretch. Monitor **EC levels**—target 1.2–1.8mS for flowering plants.”  

---

### **3.2 Environmental Stressors: The Silent Killers of Yield**  

**Overwatering**  
- **Symptoms**: Yellowing starts at leaf bases; soil remains spongy weeks post-watering.  
- **Fix**:  
  1. Implement a **24-hour irrigation gap test**.  
  2. Use a **moisture meter** (target 30–50% saturation).  
- **AI Tip**: “Soil **EC >2.3mS** = root burn risk. Dilute nutrients with distilled water to 60% strength.”  

**pH Imbalance**  
- **Symptoms**: Yellow speckling on new growth; slow nutrient uptake.  
- **Fix**:  
  1. Adjust soil pH to 6.3–6.8 using **aluminum sulfate** (1g/L).  
  2. Recheck pH after 24 hours with **calibrated strips** or a digital meter.  

---

### **3.3 Pathogens & Pests: Rapid Response Required**  

**Botrytis (Gray Mold)**  
- **Symptoms**: Fuzzy gray mold on yellow leaves; decaying stench.  
- **Fix**:  
  1. Turn up **air exchange** to 800CFM.  
  2. Remove infected material >2 inches from the plant.  
- **AI Alert**: “Botrytis strikes at RH >65%. Use dehumidifiers with 45–55% RH targets.”  

**Spider Mites**  
- **Symptoms**: Fine webbing on leaf undersides; stippled yellow spots.  
- **Fix**:  
  1. Apply **Neem oil + garlic spray** every 48 hours for 5 days.  
  2. Reintroduce **predatory mites** (Phytoseiulus persimilis) for long-term control.  

---

### **3.4 Light Stress: Burn or Insufficient Exposure?**  

**Light Burn**  
- **Symptoms**: Tips curl upward and yellow within 48 hours of light boost.  
- **Fix**:  
  1. Raise HPS/MH lights to **55+cm**.  
  2. Install **40% reflectivity Mylar shields** to reduce hotspots.  
- **AI Metric**: “Leaf temperatures >29°C trigger oxidative stress. Monitor with infrared thermometers.”
